# Preparatory Reading and Pre-Course Familiarisation

The course will teach all foundational material, but pre-course familiarisation with Python, notebooks, and basic data handling will make the first weeks much easier.

## Compulsory

These are the only items students are **expected** to complete before the course starts.

### 1. Surgical Data Science overview
- **[Surgical data science – from concepts toward clinical translation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2021.102306)**  
  A concise overview of the field and its main themes.

### 2. Notebook environment
- **[Welcome to Colab!](https://colab.research.google.com/)**  
  Students should be able to open and run a notebook in Google Colab.

### 3. Basic Python / tabular data familiarity
- **[The Python Tutorial](https://docs.python.org/3/tutorial/)**  
  Students should be familiar with basic Python syntax and core concepts.
- **[10 Minutes to pandas](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/user_guide/10min.html)**  
  Students should be familiar with basic dataframe operations.

### 4. MIMIC-IV orientation
- **[MIMIC-IV, a freely accessible electronic health record dataset](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01899-x)**  
  Students should understand at a high level what MIMIC-IV is and how it is used in research.

## Optional: practical preparation

These are the highest-yield optional activities for students who want to prepare further.

### Python practice
- **[Kaggle Learn: Python](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/python)**  
  A short interactive introduction to Python.

### Pandas practice
- **[Kaggle Learn: Pandas](https://www.kaggle.com/learn/pandas)**  
  Short tutorial to gain experience handling tabular data.

### SQL / BigQuery familiarity
- **[Introduction to SQL in BigQuery](https://cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/introduction-sql)**  
  A small amount of SQL knowledge will help with accessing data sources.

## Optional: surgical / clinical AI context

These are useful contextual readings, but should not be treated as required.

### Surgery-specific AI overview
- **[Application of artificial intelligence in surgery](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11684-020-0770-0)**

### UK healthcare / implementation context
- **[AI in the NHS: Shaping the future of health care](https://www.health.org.uk/events/ai-in-the-nhs-shaping-the-future-of-health-care)**  
  Optional background only; useful for policy and implementation context.

## Optional: critical reading and research literacy

These are useful for students who want a stronger research framing before the course.

### Reporting / appraisal
- **[CONSORT-AI extension](https://www.bmj.com/content/370/bmj.m3164)**  
  Useful for thinking critically about evaluation and reporting of AI studies.

### Data quality / trustworthy AI
- **[Advances, challenges and opportunities in creating data for trustworthy AI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-022-00516-1)**

## Background / reference material

These are background resources and reference texts, not expected pre-course reading.

### Books and broader references
- **[Artificial Intelligence in Medicine](https://link.springer.com/referencework/10.1007/978-3-030-64573-1)**  
  Lidströmer N, Ashrafian H, eds. Springer, 2022. 1st ed. eBook ISBN: 978-3-030-64573-1.

- **[Pandas for Everyone: Python Data Analysis](https://books.google.com/books/about/Pandas_for_Everyone.html?id=5NnPEAAAQBAJ)**  
  Chen DY. 2nd ed. Addison-Wesley Professional, 2022/2023. ISBN: 9780137891153.

- **[Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach](https://books.google.com/books/about/Artificial_Intelligence_A_Modern_Approac.html?id=cb0qEAAAQBAJ)**  
  Russell SJ, Norvig P. 4th ed. Pearson, 2021. ISBN: 9781292401171.

- **[Secondary Analysis of Electronic Health Records](https://library.oapen.org/handle/20.500.12657/28012)**  
  MIT Critical Data. 1st ed. Springer, 2016. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-319-43742-2.  
  Suggested chapter: Chapter 15, *Exploratory Data Analysis*.

### Statistics background
- **[The Art of Statistics: Learning from Data](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Art_of_Statistics.html?id=CiZeDwAAQBAJ)**  
  Spiegelhalter D. Pelican / Penguin, 2019. ISBN: 9780241258750.  
  A broad, readable introduction to statistical thinking and interpretation.

- **[An Introduction to Medical Statistics](https://books.google.com/books/about/An_Introduction_to_Medical_Statistics.html?id=fKgXCgAAQBAJ)**  
  Bland M. 4th ed. Oxford University Press, 2015.  
  A more traditional medical statistics text, especially useful for clinical research interpretation.

### General-interest background
- **[Hello World: How to Be Human in the Age of the Machine](https://www.google.co.uk/books/edition/Hello_World/72FCDwAAQBAJ)**  
  Fry H. Doubleday, 2018. ISBN: 9781473544710.

- **[Cautionary Tales – Flying Too High: AI and Air France Flight 447](https://timharford.com/2024/07/8801/)**  
  Tim Harford.

- **[The Book of Why: The New Science of Cause and Effect](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Book_of_Why.html?id=EmY8DwAAQBAJ)**  
  Pearl J, Mackenzie D. Penguin, 2018.  
  A useful introduction to causality, which is central to clinical interpretation and decision-making.
